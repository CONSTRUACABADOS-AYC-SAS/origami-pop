The core experience

Stuck on a step? Watch just that clip.

Each model has a photo for every step and a video clip trimmed to the same moment. Tap “Watch this step” and see real hands make the fold.

Pause, scrub, slow it down or go full screen, then drop straight back to the step you were on.

FAQ
Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in screen at all. Install it and every model is open.

Does Origami Pop have ads?

Yes. The app is supported by ads and has no in-app purchases.

Do I need an internet connection?

Step photos and video clips are downloaded as you fold, so you need a connection to load them; photos you recently opened are kept on your device.

Where do my photos go?

The camera is only used when you choose to photograph a finished model. Photos are saved on your phone and are not uploaded.
