FAQ
Is Origami Pop free?

It is free to download and contains ads. There is no account to create.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes, to load new guides, images and clips from our content servers. Models you have opened are cached on your device.

Does the camera upload my photos?

No. Photos you take stay on your device unless you choose to share them. The Camera permission is optional — you can pick from your gallery instead.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Chinese, German, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Vietnamese.
