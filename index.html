Full Description (2683/4000)

Learn origami the easy way. Origami Pop shows every fold step by step, with a short video clip for the exact step you are on, so you never get stuck halfway through a model. FOLD REAL PAPER, ONE STEP AT A TIME Pick a model, grab a square of paper and follow clear photo steps. Move forward and back, jump to any step from the step strip, and watch the clip for just that fold whenever a step is hard to read. Your place is saved automatically, so you can leave and come back to the same step. ORIGAMI, KIRIGAMI AND 3D ORIGAMI Browse more than 140 guided models across three catalogs: classic paper folding, kirigami paper cutting and multi-piece 3D origami. Every model is open from the start. Search by name, filter by difficulty, folding time and category, and sort by most popular or newest. Beginner picks help you choose an easy first fold. SEE WHAT YOU WILL NEED Each model shows its difficulty, number of steps, estimated folding time and paper specifications such as paper size and weight, so you can prepare before you start. A built-in Basic Folds guide explains valley, mountain, squash, petal and reverse folds in plain language. MINI FOLD GAME Take a break with a relaxing folding game. Swipe the corners toward the centre to fold the paper and reveal the picture hidden underneath. Complete levels to earn coins and collect finished pictures. SHOW OFF YOUR FOLDS When you finish a model, take a photo of your result with the in-app camera or choose one from your gallery, save it to your Creations, and share it with friends. Mark favourites to find your best models again. MADE FOR EVERYDAY USE - No account and no sign-up: open the app and start folding - Light and dark appearance, adjustable text size - Daily folding reminder you can switch on or off - Available in 12 languages PERMISSIONS AND ADS, EXPLAINED Origami Pop is free to download and contains ads. It uses the Advertising ID so ads can be served and measured, and it may show a consent form where the law requires one. The Camera permission is used only if you choose to photograph your finished fold; photos stay on your device unless you share them. The Notifications permission is used for your optional daily reminder and for messages from us, and the app restores the reminder after your phone restarts. Folding guides, images and clips are downloaded from our content servers, so an internet connection is needed to open new models.
